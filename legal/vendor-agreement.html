This Vendor Agreement ("Agreement") is between Vendkom ("Vendkom," "we") and the business applying to or listed on vendkom.com as a vendor ("Vendor," "you"). It supplements our Terms of Service with terms specific to Vendors.

1. Term

This Agreement begins once your application is approved and your listing goes live, and continues month-to-month. Either party may terminate with 14 days' written notice (email is sufficient), or immediately if the other party breaches this Agreement materially.

2. What Vendkom provides

  • A listing on the Vendkom marketplace, visible to Organizers searching your category
  • Marketing support appropriate to your plan (search placement, promotion, featured placement on Growth/Pro)
  • Routing of booking inquiries from matched Organizers
  • Assistance coordinating logistics (contracts, scheduling) for bookings made through the platform

3. Your responsibilities as a Vendor

  • Provide accurate, up-to-date information about your business, pricing, and availability
  • Respond to booking inquiries in a timely, professional manner
  • Deliver the service you're booked for as described in your listing and as agreed with the Organizer
  • Hold all licenses, permits, certifications, and insurance required for your trade under Jordanian law (e.g. food handling permits for catering, business licensing, etc.) — Vendkom does not verify or guarantee your legal compliance and this remains solely your responsibility
  • Conduct yourself professionally with Organizers and Vendkom staff

4. Fees & commission

Your plan determines your monthly fee (if any) and commission rate per booking made through Vendkom:

  • Starter — 0 JD/month, 15% commission per booking
  • Growth — 25 JD/month, 10% commission per booking
  • Pro — 75 JD/month, 6% commission per booking

Commission is calculated on the total value of a booking made through Vendkom. At this stage, invoicing and payment collection are handled manually (bank transfer or CliQ) — we'll notify you of the process and schedule directly. You may change plans at any time; the new rate applies to bookings going forward.

5. Cancellations

If a booking is cancelled by the Organizer before the event, commission is not owed on the cancelled booking. If you (the Vendor) cancel a confirmed booking without reasonable cause, we reserve the right to review your standing on the platform, up to and including removal. We'll always work with you in good faith on genuine emergencies.

6. Independent contractor status

You are an independent business, not an employee, partner, joint venturer, or agent of Vendkom. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment or partnership relationship. You are solely responsible for your own taxes, staff, equipment, and business operations.

7. Liability

You are solely responsible for the service you deliver, including its quality, safety, and any damage, injury, or loss arising from it. Vendkom is not liable for your acts or omissions in delivering a booked service. You agree to indemnify Vendkom against claims arising from your provision of services.

8. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep the other's non-public business information (pricing structures, personal contact details of matched Organizers, internal processes) confidential, and to use it only for the purpose of fulfilling bookings made through Vendkom.

9. Vetting & removal

Vendkom reserves the right to decline an application, or remove an active listing, at its discretion — including for quality complaints, policy violations, expired licensing, or inactivity — with notice where reasonably practical.

10. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan, with the courts of Amman, Jordan having jurisdiction over any dispute not resolved informally.
