Before you brief us

What to have ready

None of this is mandatory — but the more of it you know, the sharper your shortlist comes back.

A date, or a range

An exact date is best. A range like "a weekend in late September" is workable — it actually widens who's available to you.

Rough guest count

Catering and beverage pricing moves per head, so even a band like "60–80" changes the quotes meaningfully.

A budget range

The single most useful thing you can give us. It isn't used to spend it all — it's used to avoid sending you options you'd never book.

Venue or area

A confirmed venue, or the part of town you're looking at. Some vendors have travel minimums, and access matters for setup.

Which categories you need

  • Catering — full-service food
  • Food & Beverages — bar and drinks service
  • Photography — stills coverage
  • Videography — highlight reels or full coverage

A sense of the style

Formal or relaxed, plated or grazing, documentary or posed. A couple of reference photos does more than a paragraph.

FAQ

Organizer questions

Keep scrolling — each answer opens on its own as it reaches you.

01Is Vendkom free for event organizers?

Yes. Browsing, matching, and booking through Vendkom is always free for organizers. We work directly with vendors on pricing and logistics.

02What types of events does Vendkom support?

Anything from birthdays and private parties to weddings and corporate events, public or private, of any size.

03How are vendors vetted?

Every vendor application is reviewed by our team before going live, so organizers can book with confidence.

04Who handles logistics on the day of the event?

Vendkom coordinates scheduling, contracts, and communication between you and the organizer. On Pro, our team provides full event-day logistics support.

05Can I book just one vendor?

Absolutely. Plenty of organizers come to us for a single caterer or photographer. Booking more than one simply means we also coordinate them with each other.

06How far ahead should I book?

Four to eight weeks is comfortable for most events, and longer for peak wedding dates. We do handle shorter timelines — say so in your brief and we'll prioritise vendors with confirmed availability.

07How do payments work?

A deposit secures your date, and the balance is settled after the event. Everything runs through the platform, so you're not chasing separate transfers to each vendor.

08What if a vendor cancels?

Tell us immediately and we source a replacement from the same category and price band. This is precisely why we vet for reliability, and why repeated cancellations cost a vendor their placement.
