Your onboarding

Five steps to your first booking

Most vendors go from application to a live listing in about a week. Here's exactly what happens at each stage, and what we need from you.

01

Apply

Fill in the form at the bottom of this page — business name, category, the area you serve, and a link to your work. If you don't have a website, an Instagram profile or a folder of photos is fine.

Takes ~5 minutes No cost, no commitment
02

Get vetted

Our team reviews every application. We check that your work is genuinely yours, that you hold whatever licences your category requires, and that you can commit to the events you accept. Usually this is a short call.

2–3 business days Short intro call
Have ready: your trade licence or freelance permit, and — for catering and beverage vendors — your food safety permit.
03

Build your listing

We do this with you, not to you. Our team writes your listing copy, selects and edits your strongest photos, and helps you structure packages and pricing that actually convert — most vendors underprice their first draft.

1–2 days We write, you approve
Bring: at least 5 photos of real past work, and a rough idea of your minimum booking price.
04

Go live

Your listing is published and you start appearing in organizer matches for your category and area. On Growth and Pro, our marketing team begins promoting you across search, social and email the same week.

Same day as approval Marketing starts immediately
05

Take bookings

Booking requests arrive with the event details already filled in. You accept or decline. Once you accept, we handle the contract, the schedule, and coordination with the organizer and any other vendors on the job.

Respond within 24h Paid after the event
Commission is deducted only on bookings that came through Vendkom — never on your own direct clients.
03

Before you apply

What you'll need

Nothing exotic — but having these ready is the difference between being live in a week and going back and forth for a month.

A registered business

A Jordanian trade licence, company registration, or a freelance permit. We accept sole traders and small teams — you don't need to be a large company.

Proof of your work

At least five photos of real events you've delivered. Phone photos are fine if they're clear; we'll help with selection and editing.

Category permits

  • Catering & Food and Beverages — a valid food safety permit
  • Photography & Videography — your own equipment plus backup gear
  • All categories — public liability cover is recommended, not required

Clear pricing

A starting price and a rough package structure. If you're unsure, we'll work it out together in step three — but come with a number in mind.

Capacity to respond

  • Reply to booking requests within 24 hours
  • Confirm or decline before the organizer's deadline
  • Tell us early if your availability changes

What we handle for you

  • Listing copy, photo selection and placement
  • Marketing across search, social and email
  • Contracts, scheduling and deposits
  • Event-day coordination on Pro

FAQ

Vendor questions

Keep scrolling — each answer opens on its own as it reaches you.

01What does it cost to join as a vendor?

Vendors can join for free on our Starter plan, or choose Growth or Pro for more bookings, marketing support, and lower commission per booking.

02What kind of vendors can apply?

We're currently focused on food & beverage and photography, and are expanding into videography, décor, entertainment, and rentals.

03How are vendors vetted?

Every vendor application is reviewed by our team before going live, so organizers can book with confidence.

04How does the marketing support work?

On Growth and Pro plans, our marketing team promotes your listing across search, social, and email to organizers actively planning events in your area.

05Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes — upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time from your vendor dashboard. There's no long-term contract.

06Do I pay commission on my own clients?

No. Commission applies only to bookings that came to you through Vendkom. Work you win directly is entirely yours.

07When do I get paid?

After the event is delivered. Deposits are collected up front through the platform, and the balance is released to you once the organizer confirms the event went ahead.

08What if I can't take a booking?

Decline it in the dashboard and we'll route the organizer to another vendor. Just tell us early — repeated late cancellations affect your placement.
